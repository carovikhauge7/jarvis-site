JARVIS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

JARVIS is a personal assistant project that may connect to third-party services when explicitly authorized by the user.

Information accessed

When a user connects a third-party account, JARVIS may access information made available through the permissions explicitly granted by that user. The information accessed depends on the connected service and the permissions selected by the user.

How information is used

Authorized information is used only to provide the features requested by the user, such as accessing connected account information or interacting with supported content.

Authorization

JARVIS does not require users to provide their third-party account passwords directly. Access to supported services is granted through the authorization mechanisms provided by those services.

Sharing

JARVIS does not sell personal information. Information obtained through connected services is not shared with third parties except where required to provide a user-requested feature or where required by law.

Access and deletion

Users may revoke access to a connected service through that service's account or authorization settings. Users may also request deletion of information retained by JARVIS in connection with that service.

Changes

This policy may be updated as JARVIS develops or as supported services and integrations change.
